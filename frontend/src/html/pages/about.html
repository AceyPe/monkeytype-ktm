IEEE Keyboard Typing Marathon

A competition for fast typers
history

The IEEE Keyboard Typing Marathon was the first and largest speed typing competition in Egypt founded by the IEEE AAST Alexandria Student Branch in 2022, it welcomed contestants from all over the country and recognized the fastest typers nationwide.

From researchers, content writers, and programmers, to all keyboard practitioners, contestants showed off their typing speed at the competition.

In the 1 st edition in 2022, the contest had over 100 participants with prizes sponsored by MSI Gaming and AAST. After its great success and demand for a 2 nd edition, it was reorganized in 2024 with over 150 participants and more valuable prizes from the sponsors.

Now, with more IEEE Student Members globally learning about the IEEE Keyboard Typing Marathon and its value through two successful national editions, it is time we globalize it.

problem & solution
problem:

IEEE consists of hundreds of thousands of keyboard practitioners, and the world consists of millions, from researchers, programmers, content writers, students, to more roles that require frequent use of a computer keyboard, all of which quick typing speeds boost productivity. Fast and accurate typing skills is a determiner of a person’s digital skills.

Through a study in November 2023 , average typing speeds have been found to be around 52 words per minute (WPM), and trained typists would typically reach speeds of 60-90 WPM, with the top tier exceeding 100 WPM. People unfamiliar with keyboard layouts may struggle to reach 20 WPM as they need to stare at the keyboard.

solution:

The IEEE Keyboard Typing Marathon (KTM) is a global initiative under IEEE MGA SAC, founded by IEEE AAST Alexandria Student Branch in 2022 with 3 successful national editions. IEEE KTM aims to promote digital productivity by recognizing the often-overlooked talent of fast and accurate typing. With a vision to highlight this essential skill, the program’s mission is to implement a secure, engaging, and skill-building competition platform for IEEE members worldwide to practice and compete.

The program will develop a dedicated website for practice and competition with an official brand identity and social media presence, where practice will be available at all times of the year for IEEE and non-IEEE members, and the competition will be held annually for active IEEE members with defined rules and prizes.

The IEEE Keyboard Typing Marathon (KTM) is a global, 24-hour online competition that challenges IEEE Student and Professional Members to showcase their typing speed and accuracy through a 60-second typing test.

IEEE Student Members and IEEE Professional Members of all grades can participate in the IEEE Keyboard Typing Marathon. Each of the Student and Professional categories have their own leaderboard and prizes, so students compete with students, and professionals compete with professionals. This ensures a fair and engaging experience for all participants.
